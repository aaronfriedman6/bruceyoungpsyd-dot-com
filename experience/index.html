My experience

I am a licensed clinical psychologist in New York (#028420). Currently I am engaged in postdoctoral training in psychoanalysis at Columbia University’s Center for Psychoanalytic Training and Research. I completed my doctoral studies at the Ferkauf Graduate School of Psychology (Yeshiva University) and my doctoral clinical training in inpatient and outpatient settings across New York City. After completing my doctoral training I worked as a postdoctoral psychologist at Columbia University’s Counseling and Psychological Services. I studied philosophy as an undergraduate at Columbia University (and abroad at the University of Oxford), and continue to draw on phenomenological and existential frameworks in my clinical work.
