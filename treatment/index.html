Starting treatment

1. Reach out: email me at bruce@bruceyoungpsyd.com to arrange for a free brief phone call (about 15 minutes). We’ll talk a little bit about what brings you to therapy and if I might be a good fit.


2. Initial consultation sessions: a 60-minute intake meeting (sometimes a few). We’ll talk about what’s bringing you in, who you are, and where you’re coming from more broadly. This is also a chance for you to see how it feels to work with me. The selection of a therapist is worth careful consideration, and it’s important to find someone you feel a level of basic comfort with.


3. Going forward: if we both think we can work productively together, then we can begin meeting for therapy sessions. Compared to intake sessions, therapy sessions are generally less narrowly focused on information gathering, but the collaborative exploration of how therapy is going and if your goals are being achieved is an ongoing topic of consideration. I generally see patients once or twice a week for 45-50 minute sessions. I prioritize meeting in-person on the Upper West Side but also have some virtual availability.


4. Payment: I accept the Columbia Student Health Insurance Plan. I am otherwise out-of-network and charge $275 per session. I am happy to help you coordinate getting out-of-network reimbursement. Depending on your specific plan, you may be eligible to be reimbursed by your insurance for a significant portion of my fee—I can help you look into this.
