Welcome!

Deciding to start therapy can be tricky. People come to therapy for a variety of reasons, ranging from specific symptomatic concerns like depression, dissociation, and anxiety to more general hardships in broader domains of functioning, such as developing satisfying relationships with others or being productive in work. Recognizing the potential benefit of change is an important first step. Therapy can help clarify what exactly change might look like and how it might come about.

I have experience helping people dealing with depression, anxiety, body image concerns, romantic and sexual difficulties, issues at work and school, and disruptions in their sense of connection to internal and external reality, including depersonalization, derealization, and other forms of alteration of experience. My work across inpatient and outpatient settings has familiarized me with a range of approaches to assessment and intervention and with the importance of carefully tailoring treatment to patients’ unique needs, strengths, identities, and backgrounds.

I see individual adults (18 and up) for in-person and virtual therapy. My office is located on the Upper West Side of New York City, within one block of subway and bus stops on West 79th St. (between Broadway and West End).
